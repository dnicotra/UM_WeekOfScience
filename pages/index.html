Waves through two slits

Two interactive notebooks built for the Week of Science. Both run entirely in your browser — there is no server, so the first load spends a few seconds fetching a Python runtime.

Interference & diffraction of light

The far-field pattern of a double slit, as you change the wavelength, the slit width, the separation and the distance to the screen. Watch orders go missing.

A ripple tank you can start

Plane waves hit a barrier with two gaps and the circular wavelets beyond it cross and add. Press play and vary the gap between the slits.

Made with marimo, exported to WebAssembly. Source: double_slit.py and water_waves.py.
